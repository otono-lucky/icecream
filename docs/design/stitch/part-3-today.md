Design the "Today" home screen for icecream, a mobile dating app (iOS and Android, portrait phone), using the same design system as the onboarding screens already in this project.

WHO IT'S FOR
Verified single Nigerians aged 35 to 60, divorced, widowed, separated or never married, who want a serious relationship. This screen is the first thing a member sees every day after signing in. In these examples the member is a man, so he sees women; women see men.

WHAT THE SCREEN DOES
Instead of an endless swipe deck, each member gets a small, hand-picked set of people every day (5 at most), so they consider each person properly. No swiping. Tapping a card opens that person's full profile. Browsing is free.

FEEL
Calm, warm, dignified and trustworthy: a private members' club, not a nightclub. Photos lead, but it should feel like being introduced to someone, not shopping. No hearts, flames, emoji or "hot or not" energy.

VISUAL SYSTEM (same as onboarding)
- Colours: primary wine #9E3D48 for actions, warm ivory #FAF8F5 background, white cards, sand #F1ECE6 for secondary surfaces, verified green #2D6A4F (soft #DCEFE3), review amber #B45309, near-black text #1F1B18, muted text #5F5751.
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans. Body at least 17pt, names 22–24pt bold. High contrast.
- Rounded corners 16–20pt on cards, touch targets at least 48pt, generous spacing, soft shadows at most.

SCREEN CONTENT

1. Top area
- Small "icecream" wordmark in wine, top left. A round avatar of the member (tap goes to their own profile), top right.
- Greeting: "Good morning, Emeka" (changes with time of day).
- Title: "Today's introductions".
- Subline: "5 people chosen for you. New introductions tomorrow morning."
- A "Filters" pill button with a sliders icon on the right of the title row.

2. The cards (vertical scroll, one full-width card per person)
Each card shows:
- A large photo, 4:5 portrait, rounded top corners.
- Over the bottom of the photo, on a soft dark gradient: first name and age ("Chioma, 47"), and city with a small pin icon ("Lagos").
- Verification badges as small pills: "Photo verified" (green tick), and "ID verified" when they have it.
- Below the photo, on white:
  - One line of key facts separated by dots: "Divorced · Has children · Christian, practising".
  - A "Why we suggested her" chip in soft sand with a small sparkle icon, and a short reason: "Also in Lagos · Shares your faith".
  - A full-width secondary button "View profile".
Use these four women as the sample cards: Chioma, 47, Lagos (photo and ID verified); Amaka, 41, Enugu (photo verified); Funmilayo, 52, Ibadan (photo and ID verified); Ngozi, 55, Port Harcourt (photo verified). Photos: well-dressed Nigerian women of those ages, natural and dignified, never sexualised.

3. After the last card
A calm end-of-list panel: sand background, small sun icon, "That's everyone for today", "New introductions arrive tomorrow morning. Take your time with today's.", and a text button "Review people you passed on".

4. Bottom tab bar
Four tabs with clear line icons and labels: "Today" (home/sun icon, active, in wine), "Interests" (heart-handshake or star icon, with a small wine badge showing "2"), "Messages" (chat bubble), "Me" (person). White background, thin top border.

ALSO DESIGN THESE STATES
- Loading: skeleton cards (sand blocks where the photo and text go).
- Empty: brand-new member with no introductions yet: illustration, "Your first introductions are on their way", "We're finding people who fit what you're looking for. Check back tomorrow morning."
- Offline: small inline banner at the top, "You're offline. Showing today's introductions from earlier."

Keep everything consistent with the existing onboarding screens and the shared components frame.
